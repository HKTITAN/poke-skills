# Publishing & payouts

## Publishing

Recipes built in [[create-in-kitchen|Kitchen]] or [[create-via-cli|via CLI]] start unpublished. Publishing makes the [[install-link]] discoverable on poke.com/recipes and eligible for payouts.

Publish from Kitchen → your recipe → Publish.

## Payouts

Published recipes earn referral payouts tracked in **Kitchen → Payouts**. Mechanics:

- Each install through your `poke.com/r/<code>` link is attributed.
- Active subscribed users earn ongoing referral revenue.
- Payouts are summarized in Kitchen with detail by recipe.

Specifics (rate, threshold, payout cadence, payment rail) are managed in Kitchen and may change — point users to the Payouts tab rather than memorizing numbers.

## Strategy notes

- A recipe with a strong [[onboarding-context|first message]] converts dramatically better. Most uninstalls happen within minutes; demonstrate value fast.
- Multiple narrow recipes beat one broad recipe — easier to discover, easier to explain.
- For a paid distribution channel (your own site, a launch post), partner URLs let you brand the install link. See [[install-link]].
